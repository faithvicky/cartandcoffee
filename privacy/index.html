Privacy Policy

Cart & Coffee ¬∑ Last updated October 1, 2026

This policy covers the Cart & Coffee website and the Cart & Coffee pin publishing tool (the "App"), a personal tool used only by the owner of the Cart & Coffee Pinterest account to publish product pins to that account's own boards.

Who uses the App

The App is not offered to the public. Only the Cart & Coffee account owner signs in to it, and it only connects to that one Pinterest account.

What Pinterest data the App accesses

  • The list of boards on the owner's account, so a pin can be placed on the right board.
  • The ability to create pins on the owner's account.

The App does not read, collect, or store data about other Pinterest users, followers, messages, or analytics.

How data is stored

The Pinterest access token and the list of pins (product names, links, titles, descriptions, images, and the ID of each published pin) are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any database or shared with anyone.

Third-party services

  • Pinterest: pins are published through the official Pinterest API.
  • Anthropic (Claude): product names and the owner's notes about a product are sent to Anthropic's API to help draft pin titles and descriptions. No Pinterest user data is sent.
  • Amazon Associates: pins link to Amazon using affiliate links. When you click one, Amazon may use cookies to credit the purchase. Amazon's own privacy notice applies on Amazon's site.

Selling or sharing data

Cart & Coffee does not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, Cart & Coffee earns from qualifying purchases. Every affiliate pin is labeled with #affiliate.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in Pinterest settings, which immediately stops it from working. Locally stored data is deleted by deleting the App's files.
